Your Wacom

The tip writes with pressure. For a pen with two side buttons, sensible Wacom driver assignments are:

Lower side button
Right click → hold + touch or drag to delete · double-click to undo
Upper side button
Middle click → hold to scroll · double-click to redo
Zoom with the next tip contact
Tap lower, then upper, quickly while hovering · then tap and drag: up zooms in, down zooms out
ExpressKeys, if available
Undo · Redo · V · Tab

Button names follow the assignments in your Wacom driver. The app doesn’t change those assignments.
