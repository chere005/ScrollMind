Your Wacom

The tip writes with pressure. For a pen with two side buttons, sensible Wacom driver assignments are:

Lower side button
Right click → hold to erase
Upper side button
Middle click → hold to scroll
ExpressKeys, if available
Undo · Redo · V · Tab

Button names follow the assignments in your Wacom driver. The app doesn’t change those assignments.
